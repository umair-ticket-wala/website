Common questions

How does booking work?

Send us your city, travel month and number of travellers. We reply on WhatsApp with today's best rate and a full price breakdown. Once you confirm, we guide you through documents and payment step by step.

Why don't you show a fixed price on the website?

Airline fares and hotel rates change every day, sometimes every hour. Instead of showing an old price, we send you the real price for your dates so there are no surprises later.

How does the free fare alert work?

Tell us your route, travel month and budget. When we find a fare below your budget, we message you on WhatsApp. It's free and there's no obligation to book.

Will I get a receipt for my payment?

Yes. Every payment gets a written receipt, and your ticket, visa and hotel confirmations are shared with you before you travel.
