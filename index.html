Common questions

Are the fares on this website live?

Yes. Fares are checked against current airline prices and refreshed through the day. They are estimates, because airlines change prices often — we confirm the exact price on WhatsApp before you pay.

How does booking work?

Pick a fare or package and tap the WhatsApp button. We reply with today's exact rate and a full price breakdown. Once you confirm, we guide you through documents and payment step by step.

How does the free fare alert work?

Tell us your route, travel month and budget. When we find a fare below your budget, we message you on WhatsApp. It's free and there's no obligation to book.

Will I get a receipt for my payment?

Yes. Every payment gets a written receipt, and your ticket, visa and hotel confirmations are shared with you before you travel.
